Privacy Policy

This policy explains how runtime-dmc-pi handles Google user data for its owner-operated personal workflows.

Service and operator

runtime-dmc-pi is a private, single-user task service operated for its owner's personal use. It is not offered to the public. Privacy questions can be sent to the support address displayed on its Google OAuth consent screen.

Google data accessed

Depending on the features the owner enables, the service may access:

  • Messages, headers, and message metadata in the dedicated agent Gmail account.
  • The ability to send email from that dedicated agent account.
  • Events and event metadata from calendars explicitly shared with the agent account.
  • Basic Google account identity information needed to confirm that authorization belongs to the configured agent account.

Authorization for the agent account does not provide access to other Google accounts owned by the same person. Messages forwarded into the agent mailbox or calendars shared with it are accessible because they have been deliberately placed within the agent account's authorized view.

How data is used

Google user data is used only to provide owner-requested or owner-configured functions, including:

  • Searching for and reading relevant messages.
  • Answering questions or preparing summaries based on selected messages.
  • Drafting email and sending it under explicit approval or a narrowly configured scheduled policy.
  • Providing reminders or recommendations based on explicitly shared calendar events.

Google user data is not used for advertising, sold, or used to build profiles for unrelated purposes.

Processing and disclosure

Data is first retrieved and constrained by software running on owner-controlled hardware. When the owner requests analysis or generation, selected content may be processed by an AI service, including OpenAI services. Requested results or limited excerpts may be delivered to private Discord channels controlled by the owner.

OAuth client secrets, refresh tokens, and access tokens are not intentionally sent to AI services or Discord. Data is not disclosed to other people except when the owner explicitly directs an outbound communication.

Storage and retention

  • OAuth credentials are encrypted at rest on owner-controlled hardware and rendered locally with restrictive file permissions.
  • Mailbox content is retrieved on demand. The Gmail reader is designed not to persist full message bodies in routine operational logs.
  • Operational metadata, owner-requested summaries, and private Discord messages may be retained so the owner can review actions and results.
  • Attachments are not downloaded or processed unless a future feature is deliberately enabled and this policy is updated.

Security and owner control

Access is limited through OAuth scopes, encrypted credential storage, private communication channels, read-only defaults, recipient controls, and human approval for external actions. No security control can eliminate all risk.

The owner can revoke access from the Google Account permissions page, remove the locally stored refresh token, stop the service, or delete retained local and Discord records.

Google API policy

Use and transfer of Google API data comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Changes

This policy will be updated before the service materially expands how it accesses, uses, stores, or shares Google user data. The effective date at the top identifies the current version.
